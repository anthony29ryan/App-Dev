## My Favorite Anime

Just a quick list of my all-time favorite series.

> "My magic is never giving up!" - *Asta, Black Clover*

---

### My Top 3:

1.  **Black Clover**
    * All about **Asta** on his quest to become the *Wizard King*.
2.  **One-Punch Man**
    * The story of `Saitama`, a "hero for fun" who's just too strong.
3.  **Demon Slayer (鬼滅の刃)**
    * Breathtaking animation and a great story of family.
    * *Favorite Character:* Tanjiro Kamado
